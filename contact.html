Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about everyday pharmacy support and using VitaCare.

How can I check whether a product is available?

Send us an enquiry with the product name. Our team can help confirm current availability and any relevant details.

Can I ask for guidance before choosing a product?

Yes. Contact the pharmacy team with your general question. For personal medical advice, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

What should I know about prescription items?

Prescription items are subject to the required prescription review and applicable pharmacy checks.

How do I ask about delivery?

Contact our team to confirm service areas, timing, availability and any delivery charges before placing an order.
